# 🎮 Redfox: Whack a Fox

Welcome to **Redfox: Whack A Fox**, an exciting reflex-based arcade game where your mission is simple but intense — **Whack those Foxes before they escape!**  

This project is built using **Unity** and includes an **Android build** for mobile users. Follow the instructions below to play the game, explore its rules, and learn how to build and install it on your Android device.

---

## 📜 Game Rules

Before diving in, please take a moment to go through the rules. Understanding them will help you maximize your score and improve your reflexes.

### 🕹️ How to Play:
- Foxes, Racoons and Treasures randomly pop out of holes.
- Tap on A Fox Twice before it goes back underground.
- Avoid the Racoons with Bombs so as to lose lose 5 points.
- Tap the Treasure Chest when they open to gain more points.
- Every successful Whack scores 10+ pointS.
- Missing a mole (or tapping a Racoon) may cost you time or lives.
- Game ends when the timer runs out.

### 🎯Expand on the Game Modes:
- **Arcade Mode**: Unlimited play with increasing speed.
- **Time Attack**: Score as many hits as possible within a limited time.
- **Survival Mode**: Miss three moles and it’s game over.

## 📱 Installing the Android Build

> ⚠️ Android installations from outside Google Play require allowing "Install from Unknown Sources" in your device settings.

### How to Install:
1. **Transfer the APK** to your Android phone via USB, Bluetooth, or cloud services (Google Drive, Dropbox, etc.).
2. Open the file on your device.
3. Accept installation prompts and permissions.
4. Once installed, launch the game and start whacking!
